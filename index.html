What to say when you call

Four short steps. Can they help, what does it cost, how soon?

  1. Say what you need“I have a toothache,” “I need a tooth pulled,” or “I need dentures.” One sentence is enough.
  2. Say your insurance situationNo insurance, Medicaid, or private. It decides which clinics can take you.
  3. Ask the total cost up front“What will this cost me in total?” Free clinics may need an application first, like Harmony.
  4. Ask walk-in or appointmentAnd if they are full, ask: “Who else should I call?” Then try the next clinic on this page.
Say it like this: “Hi, I don’t have dental insurance and I need [a tooth pulled / a filling / a cleaning]. Do you take new patients, what would it cost me, and what’s the soonest I can be seen?”

What to bring

Clinics commonly ask for these. Bring what you have; if you are missing something, call and ask rather than skipping the visit.

🪪
Photo ID

Driver’s license or any photo ID you have.

🏠
Proof of address

A bill or letter with your name and address. Harmony serves Pulaski County residents.

💵
Proof of income

Pay stub, benefits letter, or similar. Sliding-scale fees are set from this.

💳
Insurance card

Medicaid or any insurance card, if you have one. No card? Say so; most clinics here serve the uninsured.

💊
Medicine list

Every medicine you take, plus allergies. Write it on paper if that is easier.

🦷
Your dentures

For repairs, relines, or replacements, bring the denture itself, even if it is broken or in pieces.
